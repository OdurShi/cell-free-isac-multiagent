## E10 online tracking (`e10_online_tracking.json`)

D = 24 APs (planned deployment), 15 users, 4 UAVs (one per flight level), 240 slots of 0.5 s, 20 scenarios; c_tx = 0.004, c_rx = 0.002.  All controllers start from the reference solution of slot 0.  'Best known' is, per slot, the best utility of any controller.  The tracking gain captured is 0% for the static assignment and 100% for the best known.  A delay of d slots applies the solution computed for slot t - d at slot t.
